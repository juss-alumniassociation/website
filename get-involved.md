JUSSAA works best when alumni participate in ways that feel useful and manageable. You can reconnect, take part, or share experience without there being a single expected route.

## Reconnect

Find old classmates, rediscover shared memories and stay connected with alumni across generations and around the world. Follow JUSSAA through the social links on our [Contact page]({{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}).

## Take part

Alumni can help at events, volunteer time for Association activities, or support fundraising and community initiatives. Email us to start a conversation.

[Ask about volunteering](mailto:{{ site.data.social.email }}?subject=JUSSAA%20-%20Volunteer)

## Share experience

There may be opportunities to mentor students, share professional knowledge, and contribute to careers or workplace conversations. These activities are arranged by conversation at present.
