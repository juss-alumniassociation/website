Welcome home

A lifelong alumni community

Whether you left the school recently or many years ago, JUSSAA exists to keep alumni connected to one another and to the school community that helped shape them.

We celebrate alumni stories and achievements, create opportunities to reconnect, and help alumni contribute their experience, ideas and support to current students and future generations.

Katherine Norton
Chairperson, JUSSAA

Be part of it

Connect. Contribute. Celebrate.

JUSSAA works best when alumni participate in ways that suit their time, interests and experience. There is no single way to be involved.

01

Reconnect

Find old classmates, rediscover shared memories and stay connected with alumni across generations and around the world.

03

Mentor students, share professional knowledge, and help create career, internship and networking opportunities.

Our story

Memories that span generations

Some of the school’s earliest alumni remember walking to the new Junior School building in 1950 with their books in hand, planting trees and marking the opening day together. Those personal memories are part of a much larger shared history that JUSSAA wants to preserve and celebrate.
