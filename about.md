JUSSAA, the Junior & Senior School Alumni Association, exists to keep alumni connected to one another and to the school community that helped shape them. Whether you left recently or many years ago, you are part of a community that continues beyond graduation.

We celebrate alumni stories and achievements, create opportunities to reconnect, and help alumni contribute their experience, ideas and support to current students and future generations.

## A welcome from our Chairperson

## Our mission and vision

Our mission is to strengthen lifelong connections among alumni and between alumni and the school. We want JUSSAA to be a welcoming community where shared memories, experience and support can benefit future generations.
